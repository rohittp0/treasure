✦   PRIVACY POLICY

What we keep.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy covers TreasureHunch on the web at hunch.rohittp.com and in the TreasureHunch apps for Android and iPhone. You can play as a guest without an account; most of what is listed below applies only once you sign in.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number, if you sign in with your phone number or verify it for the daily quest alert. It is used to send sign-in codes, to sign you in, and to send the daily alert if you opted in. It is never shown to other explorers.
  • Your Google account name, email address and photo, if you sign in with Google. The email is used to tell you when a friend challenges you by username.
  • Your account ID (uid), a random identifier for your account.
  • Your username, which is public, and when you accepted these Terms.
  • Gameplay: the hunts and daily quests you play, your results, times, clues used, streak and explorer map, your leaderboard entries, and the friend hunts you hide or play, including the clues you write.
  • Notification tokens for your browser or device, if you allow notifications.
  • Reports and blocks you make, and reports about you.
  • Crash and error reports: what went wrong and the device or browser it happened on (PostHog on the web, Firebase Crashlytics in the apps).
  • Analytics: which screens and buttons you use and how hunts go, with your device or browser type, app version, and the ad or link that brought you. We use PostHog, Firebase (Google Analytics) and Meta. PostHog receives your account ID and username so we can follow a session; Google and Meta receive only whether you are signed in, never your number, email or username.

What other explorers see

Your username, your explorer map and your leaderboard places are public. The explorer you play a friend hunt with sees your name and the hunt, and anyone with the hunt's link can see a finished result. Your number and email are never shown.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use these services to run the game, and they process data for us:

  • Google Firebase: accounts, database, notifications, hosting, crash reports and analytics;
  • Twilio and WhatsApp: sending sign-in codes and daily alerts to your number;
  • Resend: sending challenge emails;
  • PostHog and Meta: analytics, error reports and measuring our ads.

We may also share data when the law requires it, or to deal with abuse reported under the Terms of Use.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When an account is deleted, its data is erased; friend hunts you played with others stay for them, showing you as "Former explorer". Reports may be kept to protect other explorers. If an account is banned, its number stays linked to the banned account so it can't sign up again.

Your choices

  • Stop WhatsApp alerts by replying STOP or from your settings.
  • Turn notifications off in your settings or on your device.
  • Delete your account in the app where it offers Delete account, or ask us from the Support page to delete it and your data, or to send you a copy.

Children

TreasureHunch is a general-audience game. If you believe a child has given us personal data without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the game before it applies.
