✦   SUPPORT

Need a hand?

Write to us at SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER. Include your username if you have one, and the hunt code if your question is about a friend hunt.

Report an explorer or content

The fastest way is in the game: every received challenge, friend-hunt result and explorer map has Report and Block. You can also email us. We act on every report within 24 hours, removing the content and banning the explorer who posted it, as the Terms of Use say.

Block someone

Tap Block on their challenge, a friend-hunt result with them, or their map. Their challenges and your friend hunts with them disappear at once, and their challenges no longer reach you.

Sign-in codes on WhatsApp

Codes come from TreasureHunch on WhatsApp. If one doesn't arrive, check the number and country code, wait a minute and tap Resend code. You can ask for a few codes each day.

Stop the daily quest alert

Reply STOP to the WhatsApp message, or switch it off under My logs. Your number stays linked so you can still sign in with it.

Delete your account

Use Delete account in the app where it is offered, or email us from the address you want deleted, with your username, and we will delete your account and its data. See the Privacy Policy for what is kept.
